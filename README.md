🌱 About Kisaan Vaani

Kisaan Vaani is a voice-first agricultural AI assistant designed to make agricultural information easier to access for users who face barriers related to language, literacy, and complex digital interfaces.

Instead of requiring farmers to navigate menus, type queries, or interact with an English-first interface, Kisaan Vaani enables them to:

🎙️ Speak naturally → Get a simple spoken answer

The experience is designed around the farmer — not the interface.

⸻

🎯 The Problem

Agricultural information may already exist online, but availability does not guarantee accessibility.

Farmers can still face challenges such as:

* 🌐 Language barriers
* 📱 Complex application interfaces
* ⌨️ Dependence on typing
* 📚 Literacy-related friction
* 🔎 Difficulty finding relevant information
* 🗣️ Limited access to natural vernacular interaction

The real problem is not simply:

“Does agricultural information exist?”

It is:

“Can a farmer easily access and understand it?”

⸻

🤖 Why Sarvam AI?

Sarvam AI forms the vernacular language layer of Kisaan Vaani.

It enables the system to move beyond an English-first interaction model and support a more natural way of communicating with users.

Sarvam’s role in the system

Farmer's Speech
       ↓
Vernacular Language Layer
       ↓
Query Understanding
       ↓
Agricultural Information
       ↓
Conversational Response

This makes language accessibility a core part of the product, rather than a decorative AI feature.
